## 🗺️ Why JARVIS-Lite First?

### Problem 1: The Ambitious Roadmap Lacks Direction

**Original 10 ideas:**
- Idea #1: Model store (infrastructure)
- Idea #2: Voice autonomy (complex)
- Idea #3: Mesh networking (very complex)
- Idea #4-9: Various advanced features
- Idea #10: Ties everything together (last)

**Issue**: No clear "shipped product" until week 37+

**JARVIS-Lite fix**: MVP in week 8, then iterate

---

### Problem 2: Infrastructure vs Product

**Ambitious approach**: Build infrastructure first
- Model store → sync → mesh → caching → federated learning
- **Result**: Great plumbing, no product

**Pragmatic approach**: Build product first
- Core brain → skills → tools → memory → **ship**
- **Result**: Users can use it, then optimize plumbing later

---

### Problem 3: Feedback Loop

**Ambitious**: 37 weeks → release → "sorry, users don't want mesh networking"

**Pragmatic**: 8 weeks → release → "users love exams, want better formatting"

**Better**: Get real feedback early, iterate fast

## 🎯 The Decision Matrix

### When to Choose Ambitious (10 ideas):
- ✅ You have a team of 5-10
- ✅ You have 6+ months of funding
- ✅ You know exactly what users want
- ✅ You're building for a known use case with proven demand

### When to Choose Pragmatic (1 focus):
- ✅ You're 1-2 people (you!)
- ✅ You want to validate ideas fast
- ✅ You're unsure what users need
- ✅ You want to ship this quarter
- ✅ You want sustainable maintenance

**For JARVIS-Lite: Pragmatic is the right choice**
